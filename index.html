Certification Decision Assessment

FOR GERMAN E-COMMERCE SMEs

START
Grounded in the Technology–Organization–Environment framework (Tornatzky & Fleischer, 1990) and the empirical findings of Ebeleme (2026), E-Commerce Certification for German SMEs: A Study of Online Reputation and the Perceived Value of the Certification Process, Leipzig University.
This tool is a decision-support heuristic, not a validated predictive instrument or professional advice. See Figure 8.1 and Section 8.4 of the source thesis for full grounding and boundary conditions.
